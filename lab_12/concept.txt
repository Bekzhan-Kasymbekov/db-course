This is an online learning platform design.

Entities:
- Instructors
- Students
- Courses
- Enrollments
- Lessons
- Assignments
- Submissions
- Payments
- Reviews

Relationships:
- One instructor - many courses

- One course - many lessons
- One course - many enrollments
- One course - many assignments
- One course - many reviews
- One course - one category
- One course - many payments

- One student - many enrollments
- One student - many submissions
- One student - many payments
- One student - many reviews

- One assignment - many submissions

- One category - many courses
